Motion access denied

      
Wings is flown by tilting the phone, so it needs motion access. Close the app completely, open it again, tap Fly and choose Allow.

    

  


  

    
Rotate your phone

    
Wings is flown in landscape. If it won't rotate, turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center.
