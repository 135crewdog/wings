# Where Hawaii comes from

The tiles in `terrain/hawaii/` are made by `make-hawaii.py` from these sources. The app credits the photographs in small print along the foot of the pause screen's map.

- **The ground's shape:** U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program, 1/3 arc-second (about 10 m) digital elevation model. Public domain. <https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program>
- **The pictures:** ESA WorldCover 2021 Sentinel-2 median L2A (RGBNIR) composite, 10 m. © ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by ESA WorldCover consortium. Licensed under CC BY 4.0, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. Modified: reprojected, the islands moved closer together, the colours lifted, and cut into tiles. <https://esa-worldcover.org>
- **Where the water is:** ESA WorldCover 2021 land cover map, 10 m, class 80 (permanent water). Same copyright and licence as the pictures.
- **The runways:** OurAirports, public domain. <https://ourairports.com/data/>
